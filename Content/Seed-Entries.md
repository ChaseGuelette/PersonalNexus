# Seed Entries

> Part of [[Content]]

The first real entries to anchor the nexus. Without seed content, the graph is empty and doesn't prove anything. With well-chosen seeds, the MVP looks alive on launch.

## Goals for Seed Content

1. **Demonstrate the visual concept** — when Chase first opens his nexus, he sees real, meaningful nodes, not lorem ipsum
2. **Cover multiple node types** — entries + photos + people + ideas + places — so the visual variety is on display
3. **Have at least one rich connection example** — like the Switzerland-fragments-authors-mirrors web that already exists in concept
