# Design

> Part of [[BRAIN-INDEX]]

The visual canon for PersonalNexus. Every aesthetic decision lives here. When [[builder]] is implementing a visual element, this folder is the spec.

The aesthetic is non-negotiable. PersonalNexus is art that happens to also be software, not the other way around. (See [[Vision/Non-Goals]].)

## Key Files
- [[Visual-Language]] - Palette, motifs, materials, light, particles
- [[Backgrounds]] - Star fields, color shifts, the ticking watch, designable backdrops
- [[Node-Aesthetics]] - How memories, photos, ideas, and people are rendered
- [[Navigation-Model]] - Zoom + pan + focus + context (Skyrim-style)
- [[Moodboard]] - Pointers into `images/` for visual references
