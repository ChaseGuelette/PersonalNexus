Lets just start with me writing everything I want this project to be since I've never really articulated it before. 

I want to build a place where I can look at all of my thoughts and ideas and journaling. i want to be able to put images and videos and art and text all in one place. I've imagined it as a spinning orb in space with dna style helixes coming out, and the helixs have connections on them like you might see with dna. The connections are moments and memories and pictures and they are all connected to one antoher. There would be association matricies that relate different thoughts and emotions and moments in my life. And then from there, you have the actual things that I;ve written about, and then I kinda wanted to do this poetry style analysis where I can look at the things I've written about and then analyse them.
