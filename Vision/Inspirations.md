# Inspirations

> Part of [[Vision]]

The visual and conceptual ancestry of PersonalNexus. Reference images live in `images/` at the project root (see [[Assets]]).

## Visual Inspirations (in `images/`)

### DNA Helixes (`dna.jpg`, `helix.png`)
The connecting structures between nodes. Glowing blue/purple, electric. Helixes are not just decoration — they ARE the relationship lines. Their twist conveys movement; their base-pair connection points are where individual memories live.

### Sacred Geometry — Icosahedron (`icosahedron.jpg`, `helix.png`)
The "core orb" likely renders as an icosahedron or another Platonic solid — clean lines, sacred-looking, suggestive of structure beneath the chaos. The helix.png reference combines an icosahedron core with DNA helixes radiating out — the cleanest visual articulation of the whole concept so far.

### Skyrim Skill Tree (`skyrimskillselection.jpg`)
The navigation model. Constellations of glowing nodes against a deep nebula background. You can zoom into one constellation while always seeing the others. Selection is reverent, not clicky.

### Cosmic Watch (`5557689e-c041-4cb1-a0e5-8334109f41d8.png`, `watchface.jpg`, `watchface.avif`)
The background of the experience. A real-time ticking mechanical watch face floating in deep nebula — designable, swappable, evolving. The Holzkern watch with wood/blue/galaxy face captures the feeling: ornate, tactile, organic-meets-precise.

### Constellation Portrait (`fire_ahh_oldman.png`)
The aesthetic for "person nodes." A face framed in constellations and sacred geometry. Mom-node, Dad-node, Grandparent-node — each a portrait wreathed in cosmic ornament.

### BrainTree 2D (`braintreeos2d.png`)
What 2D graph visualization looks like — a useful baseline for what we're *evolving past*. PersonalNexus is what happens when you take this 2D web and lift it into living 3D.

## Conceptual Inspirations

- **Memory palaces** — the ancient mnemonic technique of placing ideas in spatial structures
- **Borges' "The Library of Babel" / "The Aleph"** — the idea of a single point that contains all knowledge
- **Italo Calvino's "Invisible Cities"** — places as repositories of memory and self
- **Carl Jung's archetypes** — the symbolic web underneath personal experience
- **Illuminated manuscripts** — the marriage of text and ornament; reading as ceremony

## Anti-Inspirations
What PersonalNexus is NOT trying to be:
- Notion / Obsidian / Roam (productivity-coded knowledge tools)
- Social media (no audience but the self)
- Clinical mind-mapping software (no business-process aesthetic)
- A flat-modern SaaS app
