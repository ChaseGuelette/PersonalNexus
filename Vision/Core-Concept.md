# Core Concept

> Part of [[Vision]]

## The Idea, In Chase's Own Words

> "I want to build a place where I can look at all of my thoughts and ideas and journaling. I want to be able to put images and videos and art and text all in one place. I want to be able to link those ideas like a knowledge graph — I am trying to build an evolving image of me. I want to be able to look at this, this nexus, and go 'that is Chase, or at least what I've written about myself'."

> "Somewhere between a journal and a notepad and a museum of myself, and I wanna combine that with it also being a piece of art that you can interact with."

> "I guess what I am trying to build here is like a representation of my brain. Not in a productivity or remember way, but in a 'make sense of the flurry of things happening in my head' way."

## The Visual Image

A spinning orb in space. DNA-style helixes radiate outward. The connections along those helixes — like base pairs — are moments, memories, photos, ideas. Everything is connected to something. Pictures of family link to the person; the person links to a thousand other memories. The Switzerland diary entry is connected to *authors → mirrors → fragments → family → Switzerland photos → Mom*. The entry deviates into a poem about how fantasy authors can't end series because their characters are pieces of themselves; that thread continues to other entries about identity and writing.

## The Topology

Each person has their own nexus. Chase's nexus connects to Mom's nexus. Chase is close to Mom; Mom is close to her parents; Chase is a little further from those grandparents. Relational distance = spatial distance. The graph isn't just *displayed* in 3D — it *lives* in 3D, with placement carrying meaning.

## The Mode

Not productivity. Not memory aid. **Self-witnessing.** A place to see, in one image, the shape of one's interior life.
