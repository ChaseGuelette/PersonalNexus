# Non-Goals

> Part of [[Vision]]

What PersonalNexus is *explicitly not*. Important for keeping scope honest and the aesthetic uncompromised.

## Not a Productivity Tool
PersonalNexus is not for getting things done. No todo lists, no GTD, no Pomodoro, no Kanban. If a feature exists to make Chase more efficient, it doesn't belong here.

## Not a Note-Taking App
This isn't Notion, Obsidian, Roam, Logseq, or Apple Notes. We're not optimizing for fast capture or quick search across a structured document tree. The graph is artistic and reflective, not an information retrieval system.

## Not a Social Network
The audience is Chase. There is no sharing feed, no "publish," no comments, no profiles for others to view. Cross-nexus connections (Chase ↔ Mom) are *Chase's* representations of others, not other people's accounts.

## Not a Diary App
There are diary apps. PersonalNexus contains diary content but is a fundamentally different artifact — it's the spatial-graph-art that *holds* the diary, not a chronological list of entries.

## Not Built for Speed of Capture
Adding an entry should feel ceremonial, not frictionless. If we have to choose between fast-input and meaningful-input, choose meaningful.

## Not Generic
This is not a platform that other people will use. We're not building a multi-tenant system. There is one user. The aesthetic, schema, and tags are all tailored to Chase. (If others end up wanting it, *that* is a separate project.)

## Not Done Without the Art
A version of this that has all the data plumbing but looks like a regular flat-modern app is a failure. The visuals are not polish — they are the product.
