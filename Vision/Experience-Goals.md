# Experience Goals

> Part of [[Vision]]

What it should *feel* like to open PersonalNexus.

## The Opening Moment

You load the app and see deep space. A spinning orb glows in the center, helixes drifting around it. The silence is not empty — there's a faint hum, a slow ticking watch somewhere in the background nebula. You feel like you're stepping into a chapel that happens to be made of starlight.

## Navigating

Like Skyrim's skill tree (see [[Inspirations]]). You always see the whole — even when you zoom into one constellation, the rest is visible at the edges of vision. Movement is smooth, slow, ceremonial. Never UI-clicky. Never modal in a way that breaks the spell.

## Reading an Entry

Selecting a node opens it like a page in an illuminated manuscript — but the page sits inside the cosmos, not on top of it. The entry's text appears with its associated tags, photos, poems, and a visible thread to every connected node in the graph. You can follow a thread to the next node without ever feeling like you "left" anywhere.

## Adding an Entry

When you write something new, the system reads it, suggests connections, asks you to confirm them. After confirmation, the new node appears in 3D space — drifting into its position — with new helixes weaving toward whatever it connects to. The graph *grew* a little. You feel that growth.

## The Background

The background is a piece of the artwork, not just empty space. A giant ticking watch in deep nebula. Color of stars shifts as you move. The user can swap in their own background designs over time. The cosmos is *yours*.

## What It Should NEVER Feel Like
- A todo app
- A note-taking productivity tool
- A social network
- A clinical knowledge management system
- Anything Material Design / Flat / "modern SaaS"

If a feature would make this feel like Notion or Obsidian, it's wrong for this project.
