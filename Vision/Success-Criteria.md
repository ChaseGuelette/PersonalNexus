# Success Criteria

> Part of [[Vision]]

How we know we're getting somewhere. These are the milestones that mean the vision is becoming real.

## MVP-Level Success

1. **Visual proof of concept**: A spinning orb in 3D space with at least one DNA helix and 5 connected nodes. Loads at 60fps. Looks unmistakably like the [[Inspirations]] (cosmic, glowing, not-a-todo-app).
2. **Real seed content**: At least one real journal entry (e.g., the Switzerland entry) is in the system, with manually attached tags and connections.
3. **Navigation feels right**: Zooming into a node and back out feels ceremonial, not clicky. Skyrim-style focus + context.
4. **It makes Chase feel something** when he opens it. If it doesn't, the MVP isn't done — even if every feature works.

## Stretch (post-MVP)

1. **Automatic association suggestions**: New entries get tag and connection suggestions from the embedding model.
2. **Cross-nexus connection**: A second nexus (Mom's, or a "Friends" nexus) exists and connects to Chase's, with relational spatial distance working.
3. **Designable backgrounds**: Chase can swap in custom background designs (e.g., the ticking watch).
4. **Image + video ingestion**: The full multi-modal pipeline is wired up.
5. **Poetry-style analysis**: An entry can be analyzed for themes/symbols and connections to existing nodes.

## The Real Test

When Chase opens PersonalNexus and thinks: *"yes — that is me, or at least the part of me I've written down so far"* — the project has succeeded.

Everything else is in service of that moment.
